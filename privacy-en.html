Privacy

Effective October 20, 2026

1. What we collect

  • Sign-in details: when you log in with Google, the email address, name, profile picture address and account ID that Google provides; with Kakao, the nickname, profile picture address, account ID and, if you agree to share it, the email address that Kakao provides. They are stored so you can sign in.
  • What you write: the happy day, the place, your one line, anything more you wrote, a photo if you add one, the clover's position in the meadow, and when it was written or edited.
  • Reports: which clover was reported, the reason, the reporting account, and when.

If you only look around without logging in, nothing is collected.

Only people aged 14 or older may log in and write. Clovers found to be written by younger children are deleted.

2. How it is used

  • To let each person edit and delete only their own clovers.
  • What you write and any photo are public in the meadow for anyone to read. Your name, email and profile picture are never shown; every clover appears as "someone's happiness".
  • Reports are used only to hide inappropriate clovers and for the admin to review them. No one else can see who reported.
  • Nothing is used for advertising, sold, or shared with other services.

3. Where it is kept, and for how long

Data is stored with Supabase on servers in Seoul, South Korea. Clovers stay until you delete them; deleting your account also deletes its clovers and reports.

4. Deleting

  • Under My clovers you can edit or delete your clovers at any time. Deleting a clover also deletes its photo.
  • To delete your account and everything with it, let us know through the contact below.

5. Cookies and browser storage

Browser storage keeps you signed in, remembers what you were doing while you go to log in, and keeps your display choices (language, a closed list, time, season and weather). There is no analytics or advertising tracking.

6. Weather and location

To show the current weather and season, the meadow uses the Open-Meteo weather service. It does not ask for your location: it asks for the weather in the city of your device's time zone (for example New York or Tokyo). Only if you turn on "Use my location for the weather" in the help sheet does your browser ask for your location, and then only a rough position (about 10km) is sent to Open-Meteo. Your location is never stored.

7. Contact
