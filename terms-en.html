House rules

Effective October 20, 2026

1. What this place is

saeip is where anyone can leave one line about a small happiness and read other people's. Anyone can read without an account; people aged 14 or older can log in and write.

2. Please don't post

  • Anything that puts others down or spreads hate, or abusive language
  • Advertising, promotion, or the same thing over and over
  • Information that identifies someone else (a name, contact details, an address), or photos of other people without their permission
  • Sexual or violent content, or anything illegal

The admin may hide or delete such posts even without reports, and may stop an account that keeps doing so from writing.

3. Your words and photos

  • What you write and the photos you add stay yours. By posting, you let saeip show them in the meadow for others to read.
  • You can edit or delete your clovers at any time under My clovers.
  • Only post other people's words or photos with the permission of whoever holds the rights.

4. Reports

You can report an inappropriate clover. After three reports it is hidden until the admin checks it. There is a daily limit on how many clovers and reports one person can make.

5. About the service

saeip is a small, free service run by one person. We try to keep it going, but it may change or stop without notice, and we cannot promise that clovers will never be lost. Changes to these rules will be posted on this page.

6. Contact

For questions or requests, please email qkrwngus0126@gmail.com. For personal information, see the privacy policy.
